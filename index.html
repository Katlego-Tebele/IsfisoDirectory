About Isifiso-Sakho Carpentry

Isifiso-Sakho Carpentry provides professional carpentry and interior improvement services for customers looking for attractive and practical wooden installations. We specialize in creating custom solutions that transform living spaces, from built-in kitchen units and wardrobes to TV units, bar tables, wooden flooring, and ceiling installations.

Based in Diepkloof Zone 5, Gauteng, we work closely with each client to understand their vision and deliver craftsmanship that meets their specific needs and enhances their home environment.
