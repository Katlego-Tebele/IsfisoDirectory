🚀 How to Add Your Images

  1. Take or gather photos of your completed carpentry projects
  2. Resize images to the recommended dimensions (use free tools like ResizePixel)
  3. Compress images to reduce file size (use TinyPNG - aim for under 500KB per image)
  4. Rename files to match the exact filenames shown above (e.g., kitchen.jpg, wardrobe.jpg)
  5. Place files in the correct folders:
    • Service images → assets/images/services/
    • Gallery images → assets/images/gallery/
    • Hero background → assets/images/
  6. Test your website by opening index.html in your browser
  7. Refresh browser (Ctrl+F5) if images don't appear immediately
💡 Pro Tip: Use your smartphone to take photos! Modern phone cameras produce excellent quality images perfect for websites. Just make sure there's good lighting and the space is tidy.
